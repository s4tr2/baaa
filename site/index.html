Questions Papa gets asked

macOS says the app "can't be opened" or is "damaged".

The early builds aren't notarized yet. Right-click Baaa.app in Applications, choose Open, then Open again. You only need to do this once. Or run xattr -cr /Applications/Baaa.app in Terminal.

My Mac doesn't have a notch.

Then he floats in a small black island just under the menu bar. Same Papa, same bubble.

How do I activate Pro?

After checkout, Dodo Payments emails you a license key. Open Baaa from the menu bar, Settings, Pro tab, paste the key, Activate. It works on Macs; deactivate on one to move it.

What does Baaa collect?

Nothing. Settings live in your Mac's user defaults, your picture stays in Application Support, and the only network call is checking a Pro license key with Dodo Payments if you have one. No analytics, no account, no microphone.

Can I make him say my own things?

That's Pro's "custom instructions": per reminder you write your own lines, use {name} for what he calls you, and add an English line after two slashes to show the grey translation. Or tell him to use only your lines.

Is this related to Maaa?

Baaa is inspired by Maaa, the mother version. Same love, different parent. Not affiliated.
