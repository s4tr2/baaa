Questions Papa gets asked

macOS says the app "can't be opened" or is "damaged".
The early builds aren't notarized yet. Right-click Baaapp.app in Applications, choose Open, then Open again. Once is enough. Or run xattr -cr /Applications/Baaapp.app in Terminal.

How do I activate Pro?
After checkout, Dodo Payments emails you a license key. Click the mustache in the menu bar, Settings, Pro tab, paste the key, Activate. It works on Macs; deactivate on one to move it.
What does Baaapp collect?
Nothing. Settings live in your Mac's user defaults, your picture stays in Application Support, and the only network call is checking a Pro license key with Dodo Payments if you have one. No analytics, no account, no microphone.
Can I make him say my own things?
That's Pro's custom instructions. Per reminder you write your own lines, use {name} for what he calls you, and add an English line after two slashes for the grey translation. Or tell him to use only your lines.
